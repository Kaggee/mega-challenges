#4471 · two date bugs in one ticket

owner support deskknownreviewed 2026-10-30

Since April, people have reported bookings landing on the wrong day. The desk merged the reports into #4471. The export has overlapping rows, so repeated report IDs are counted once.

The reports describe two causes. The shared parser reads every date month first, so it flips dates for day-first tenants. Separately, booking-v3 tells people to enter month first while the form interprets their input in the tenant's day-first locale. These need separate fixes.

Known reports, split by cause below. Counts use unique report IDs and the tenant's configured locale and form, not the intake tag or subject line.
